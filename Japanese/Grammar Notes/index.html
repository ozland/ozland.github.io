    Verbs of "Giving/Receiving"

    • agemasu: give (I give to you) (Go up 3 to 2, 2 to 1) (or 3 to 3)
    • kuremasu: give (you give me) (Go down 1 to 2, 2 to 3) (NOT 3 to 3)
    • moraimasu: receive (any direction)

    Past/negative is the same as "i" adjectives. Ex:

    • Raamen ga tabetai desu
    • Raamen ga tabetakunai desu
    • Raamen ga tabetakatta desu
