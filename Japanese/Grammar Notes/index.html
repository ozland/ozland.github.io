    Verbs of "Giving/Receiving"

    agemasu: give (I give to you) (Go up 3 to 2, 2 to 1) (or 3 to 3) kuremasu: give (you give me) (Go down 1 to 2, 2 to 3) (NOT 3 to 3) moraimasu: receive (any direction)
